# Welcome to the Paperclip hosted beta

Create a team of AI agents to take on projects and ongoing work. Paperclip gives your agents a workspace in the cloud and gives you one place to assign work, track progress, and review results.

This hosted beta is invitation-only.

## Get set up

1. **Sign in.** Open your invitation link and sign in with Google, GitHub, or email. Use the email address that received your invitation.
2. **Create your organization.** Give your organization a name.
3. **Name your first agent.** Your first agent will help you plan work and build your team. Give it a name, then select **Next**.
4. **Connect a model.** Choose Claude or OpenAI, then follow the prompts to connect your subscription or an API key.
5. **Finish setup.** When the Review screen says your agent is ready, select **Get started**.

## Start delegating work

Selecting **Get started** opens a chat with your first agent. Nothing starts until you agree on the work: the agent asks what you want to achieve, and you can ask it to interview you or just describe what you need. When the goal and the finish line are clear, it suggests a task for you to accept, or you can ask it to create the task outright.

Bring a task, an ongoing workflow, or a larger project. Explain the outcome you want and share relevant context. You can add agents and connect the tools they need as you build your team.

Follow progress in the task, answer questions, and review results. When your agent requests approval, review the proposed action before approving it or requesting changes.

See the guides to [managing tasks](https://docs.paperclip.ing/guides/day-to-day/issues/) and [tracking agent activity](https://docs.paperclip.ing/guides/day-to-day/dashboard/).

## Frequently asked questions

<details class="accordion">
<summary>What carries over when I connect Claude or OpenAI?</summary>
<div class="accordion-body">

Connecting Claude or OpenAI lets Paperclip use your account to run agents. It doesn’t import your existing conversations, local files, skills, or MCP connections. Add the context and connections your agents need.

</div>
</details>

<details class="accordion">
<summary>Will my agents keep working when I close my browser?</summary>
<div class="accordion-body">

Yes. Agents run in a hosted environment, so your computer can be off or offline while they work. Tasks can still pause for input, approvals, or usage limits.

</div>
</details>

<details class="accordion">
<summary>Is the hosted beta free?</summary>
<div class="accordion-body">

Yes. We won’t charge for hosting or agent environments during the beta. Your model provider’s charges and usage limits still apply.

</div>
</details>

<details class="accordion">
<summary>How much model usage should I expect?</summary>
<div class="accordion-body">

Usage depends on the model, task, context, and how often agents run. Scheduled work also uses your provider allowance or API credits.

Check your provider account for billing and subscription limits. Paperclip’s spend totals don’t show your remaining subscription allowance.

See [Costs and Budgets](https://docs.paperclip.ing/guides/day-to-day/costs/) and [Heartbeats and Routines](https://docs.paperclip.ing/guides/projects-workflow/routines/) to understand costs and when agents run.

</div>
</details>

<details class="accordion">
<summary>Can I connect my tools and repositories?</summary>
<div class="accordion-body">

Use **Connectors** to see the available connections and authorize access. Set them up in your hosted organization; connections from your local installation don’t carry over.

</div>
</details>

<details class="accordion">
<summary>Does Paperclip collect analytics while I use the beta?</summary>
<div class="accordion-body">

It can. When product analytics is on for your workspace, Paperclip uses analytics, and session recordings where they're turned on, to improve the product. Recordings hide what you type and your messages, documents, and connected accounts, but may show page names like task titles.

If you're in the EU, the EEA, the UK, or Switzerland, browser analytics and recordings don't start until you choose **Accept** on the banner at the bottom of the page. In those regions, if your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control, Paperclip treats that as a decline and doesn't show the banner. Elsewhere, analytics is on by default.

You can change your choice for this browser at any time with the **Product analytics** switch on your profile settings page. When Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control has turned analytics off in those regions, the switch stays off and can't be changed. See the [privacy policy](https://paperclip.ing/privacy/) for details.

</div>
</details>

<details class="accordion">
<summary>Can I import an existing company?</summary>
<div class="accordion-body">

Company import isn’t available in this beta. Create a new organization and set up its agents and connections.

</div>
</details>

<details class="accordion">
<summary>Does the existing documentation apply to the hosted beta?</summary>
<div class="accordion-body">

Use the [documentation](https://docs.paperclip.ing/) for product capabilities and workflows. You can skip self-hosted installation and runtime setup—we manage those for you.

</div>
</details>
